# 📚 Learneest

Learneest is a study platform focused on **planning, tracking, and real learning progress**. Its main goal is to help students organize their study time, compare planned vs. actual study sessions, and, in the future, learn together through social features.

The project starts as an individual productivity and learning tool and gradually evolves into a **study-focused social network**.

---

## 🎯 Project Goal

Help students to:

- Plan study sessions clearly
- Measure real time spent studying
- Build consistency and learning habits
- Centralize content such as summaries and flashcards
- (In the future) share progress and learn as a community

---

## 🚀 Features

### ✅ MVP (First Version)

- User authentication and registration
- Create and manage subjects
- Plan study sessions
- Integrated Pomodoro timer
- Automatic tracking of real study time
- Comparison between planned time vs. actual time

### 🧠 Features in Progress

- Flashcards per subject
- Space for summaries (simple editor)
- Study history by day/week
- Performance and consistency metrics

### 👥 Future (Social)

- Follow other students
- Share summaries and flashcards
- View friends’ progress
- Light gamification (streaks, goals, optional rankings)
